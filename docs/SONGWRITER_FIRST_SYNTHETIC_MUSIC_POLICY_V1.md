# Sway songwriter-first synthetic music policy v1

**Status:** Implemented policy. Production status is proven separately by the release-health record.

## Governing rule

Sway credits the human contribution that exists. A songwriter does not stop being a songwriter because a virtual
voice performs the song or a generative tool produces the recording.

An otherwise eligible release is not barred from sale, sharing, promotion, or discovery merely because it uses a
virtual performance or generative production. Its required master, composition, distribution, artwork, and
conditional synthetic-production rights evidence must still pass the existing independent review flow. Sway does
not treat synthetic production as proof that the creator lacks commercial rights, and this policy does not itself
create checkout, pricing, or delivery capabilities where Sway does not already offer them.

## Public creation facts

Each recording stores four separate facts:

1. lyrics authorship;
2. musical-composition authorship;
3. vocal or featured-performance method; and
4. recording-production method.

These facts must not be collapsed into one stigmatizing “AI song” label.

For a songwriter who writes every lyric and uses an original virtual persona to perform a generatively produced
recording, the public lead labels are:

- `Human-written lyrics`
- `Original virtual artist`
- `Rights checked`

Production details appear under `How this recording was made`. A release is `fully generated` only when its lyrics,
composition, performance, and production all meet the generated test. Human-written lyrics therefore prevent a
release from being classified as fully generated.

The songwriter chooses the public credit or pen name. Sway does not substitute a private account or legal identity
into the public credit.

## Conditional rights check

Synthetic performance or generative production requires recording-scoped commercial-use evidence through the
existing immutable `ai_disclosure` declaration. A different authorized reviewer must verify that evidence before the
release can become ready. A replica of an identifiable real performer also requires independently verified performer
consent. An original virtual persona is not treated as a replica. These checks apply to rights, not artistic worth.

## Promotion and discovery

Human-written virtual-artist releases receive normal release pages, profile placement, search, sharing, and discovery.
Listeners can search a title, artist, songwriter credit, track title, or creator-supplied lyric excerpt. Discovery offers
explicit filters for `Human-written lyrics` and `Original virtual artists`.

Sway does not downrank a release merely because it uses an original virtual voice or generative production.

## Community moderation

Signed-in community members may submit evidence-backed reports for:

- copied lyrics;
- unauthorized voice replication;
- unlicensed samples;
- missing commercial rights;
- incorrect creation credits;
- spam or duplicate releases;
- fake engagement; or
- impersonation.

“Uses AI,” “uses a virtual artist,” and “the creator cannot sing or play” are not report reasons.

A report requires at least 40 characters of investigable detail, is tied to an authenticated account, and cannot be
filed by the release owner against their own release. Duplicate active reports from the same account for the same
release and reason are rejected.

Reports create an auditable review record. They never remove, block, or downrank a release automatically. An
administrator must record a reasoned dismissal, escalation, or resolution; any separate release-status action remains
outside the report mutation.

## Current boundary

This policy does not change payouts, publishing splits, DSP delivery, pricing, or Sway.DIO launch status. It adds
creation credits, conditional rights readiness, public presentation, release discovery, and community-report review.
