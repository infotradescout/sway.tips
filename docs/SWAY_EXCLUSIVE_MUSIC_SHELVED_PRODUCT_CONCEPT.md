# Sway Exclusive Music — Shelved Product Concept

**Status:** Shelved for post-beta review  
**Date shelved:** August 27, 2026  
**Current priority:** Open the DJ beta and prove Sway’s live Request, Tip, and Boost loop in real rooms.  
**Implementation status:** Product concept only. Not approved for beta implementation.

## Decision

Sway Exclusive Music is a valid future product lane, but it is **not part of the opening DJ beta**.

The beta must stay focused on the shortest winning loop:

1. A DJ starts a live room.
2. The DJ shares a QR code or short link.
3. Patrons send Requests, Tips, and Boosts.
4. The DJ manages the live queue.
5. Patrons see clear request status.
6. The DJ closes the room and sees earnings and activity.

Exclusive music, artist-uploaded tracks, contributor splits, downloads, previews, music hosting, and special artist-request payments must not distract from proving that core loop.

## Core Future Concept

Sway Exclusive Music would let verified artists control music that can be discovered, unlocked, or requested through Sway before—or instead of—it being available elsewhere.

The central rule is:

> Sway may receive permission to provide exclusive access to a track, but the creator keeps ownership and controls the exclusivity.

Sway should not demand ownership of an artist’s master recording, composition, lyrics, artist name, or identity as a standard condition of participation.

## Product Structure

### Sway Music

The complete verified artist-and-track system.

It may eventually include:

- Verified artist profiles.
- Verified tracks and versions.
- Artist-controlled request availability.
- Contributor credits.
- Live request activity.
- Artist support payments.
- DJ access controls.
- Membership access.
- Release scheduling.
- Rights declarations and dispute handling.

### Sway Exclusives

Tracks released through either a temporary **Sway First** period or a deliberate **Sway Only** agreement.

### Sway Drops

Tracks temporarily unlocked for a specific event, room, venue, city, membership plan, release night, or time window.

Example:

> DJ3X Bike Night Sway Drop — available to request in tonight’s room before the public release.

### Sway Originals

This name should be reserved for music actually commissioned, financed, produced, or formally released by Sway under a separate agreement. Ordinary artist uploads should not automatically be called Sway Originals.

## Artist-Controlled Release States

Each track should have its own independently editable release state.

### Open Release

The track may be available on other services and can also be requested through Sway.

### Sway First

The track appears through Sway before its wider public release. The artist chooses the exclusive period.

### Sway Only

The track remains available only through Sway while the agreement is active. This must be a deliberate advanced choice and must never be the default.

### Private or Unreleased

The track is not public. The artist may prepare it, schedule it, or grant selected DJs early access.

### Retired

The track no longer accepts new requests, downloads, or member access. Historical transactions and activity remain preserved.

## Independent Access Controls

Release status and access permissions must be separate. An artist should be able to turn each use on or off without affecting every other use.

Possible controls include:

- Requestable in participating Sway rooms.
- Available only in selected rooms or events.
- Available only through selected DJs.
- Available to one or more membership plans.
- Available as an authorized download.
- Available to verified DJs through a private Sway crate.
- Available as a public preview.
- Available as a full member listen.
- Limited to selected dates or release windows.
- Clean version available.
- Explicit version available.
- Instrumental, remix, radio edit, or alternate version available.
- Temporarily paused.
- Permanently retired.

Turning off downloads must not automatically stop live requests. Pausing public discovery must not automatically erase a paid membership benefit already promised to existing members.

## Two-Sided Opt-In for Live Requests

A Sway artist track should only become requestable when both sides agree:

1. The artist has enabled that track for requests.
2. The DJ has enabled Sway Music requests in that room.

The DJ remains in control of the live performance and may:

- Approve or reject each request.
- Exclude explicit tracks.
- Limit genres.
- Allow only selected artists or tracks.
- Accept local artists only.
- Pause Sway Music requests during part of an event.
- Disable a track that does not fit the room.
- Require the track to be available within the DJ’s normal working setup.

Sway must not promise that a song will be played merely because the artist made it requestable.

## Future Live-Room Flow

A future strong version could work as follows:

1. An artist submits a finished track, artwork, credits, versions, release settings, and rights declarations.
2. Sway verifies the artist and reviews the submission requirements.
3. Participating DJs receive authorized access before an event through a private Sway crate or another approved delivery method.
4. The DJ prepares the track inside the setup already used for live performance.
5. Patrons see a Sway Exclusive or Sway Drop section inside the active room.
6. A patron requests the verified track and sees who the payment supports.
7. The DJ approves, plays, and completes the request.
8. Sway records the fulfilled request and allocates the approved payment shares.
9. The patron can follow the artist, join a membership, or receive release updates.

Sway should continue working beside Serato, Rekordbox, VirtualDJ, Tidal, USB drives, and other normal DJ workflows. It should not claim direct integration unless that integration is actually built and verified.

## Artist Payment Direction

The future payment rule should preserve both participants:

- The live DJ or performer keeps the normal base request payout for managing and fulfilling the request in the room.
- The verified artist or approved contributors may receive a separate **Sway Artist Share** when the track is actually fulfilled.
- The artist does not receive money merely because someone typed a song name.
- A rejected, expired, canceled, refunded, disputed, or unfulfilled request does not create an Artist Share.
- Sway must not secretly subtract the Artist Share from the DJ’s stated base request amount.
- Checkout must show the patron a clear breakdown before payment.
- Small artist payments should accumulate in a ledger and pay out in normal batches rather than creating a separate transfer for every request.

The exact Artist Share formula is **not decided** and should be tested only after the core beta proves real request behavior.

## Contributor Support

The underlying design should eventually support more than one approved recipient per track, including:

- Lyricist.
- Songwriter.
- Composer.
- Producer.
- Featured performer.
- Master owner.
- Other agreed contributors.

This matters because the person who wrote the song may not be the singer or instrumental performer.

The first public version may still begin with one verified payee per track, but the future data and payment structure should not permanently block contributor splits.

## Membership Connection

Each Sway membership plan could independently unlock selected music benefits, including:

- Early access to Sway Exclusives.
- Event-only Sway Drops.
- One or more exclusive request credits.
- Full tracks that nonmembers can only preview.
- Member-only downloads.
- Alternate versions or remixes.
- First access before a wider release.
- Voting on a future release.
- Private release announcements.

A benefit that is free to the member must not silently become unpaid work for the artist. Any required Artist Share must be funded by the membership plan’s benefit budget, a creator promotion budget, or another clearly disclosed source.

Sway should avoid a platform-wide mystery royalty pool during the first version. Direct, track-level, plan-level accounting is easier to understand, audit, and protect from manipulation.

## Ownership and Permission Guardrails

Before full-track access, downloads, or DJ distribution are allowed, the submitting artist should confirm that they have the required authority for the selected use.

The submission should cover:

- Control of the sound recording.
- Control of the composition or permission from the relevant rights holders.
- Accurate contributor credits.
- Permission for samples, beats, featured performers, and artwork.
- Permission to grant the selected Sway access or exclusivity.
- Accurate clean or explicit labeling.
- No impersonation of another artist.
- Permission for Sway to pause access and hold unsettled payments during a credible dispute.

Sway should use clear, limited agreements for the selected track, term, access, territory, payment, and exit rules. It should not rely on broad ownership grabs or vague permanent exclusivity.

## Machine-Assisted Music

Sway Exclusive status should describe where and how music is available, not demand one traditional production method.

Possible disclosure labels may include:

- Human-created.
- Machine-assisted production.
- Human-written composition with generated performance.
- Mixed production.
- Other disclosed creation method.

The creator must describe the human contribution and permissions honestly. Eligibility should depend on truthful disclosure, control of the submitted material, and compliance with Sway’s rights requirements—not simply on whether production tools were used.

## Fraud and Abuse Controls

Direct artist payments create obvious abuse risks. A future implementation must detect, block, or review:

- Artists repeatedly requesting their own tracks to manufacture payouts.
- Requests funded by connected payout accounts.
- Coordinated artist and DJ accounts cycling money.
- Large request bursts from one device or payment source.
- Tracks marked completed faster than they could reasonably be played.
- Duplicate or fake live rooms.
- Repeated disputes or chargebacks.
- False ownership claims.
- Duplicate track submissions.
- Stolen recordings or artwork.

Payments connected to suspicious activity should remain unsettled while reviewed.

## What Is Explicitly Outside the Opening Beta

The opening DJ beta should not include or promise:

- Artist track uploads.
- Sway-hosted full-track streaming.
- Exclusive downloads.
- Private DJ music crates.
- Sway First or Sway Only agreements.
- Contributor split payouts.
- Artist Share calculations.
- Automatic music recognition.
- Automatic proof that a song was played.
- Serato, Rekordbox, VirtualDJ, Tidal, or deck-control integrations.
- Replacement of normal venue or music licensing obligations.

The beta should use ordinary patron-entered song requests and the existing DJ workflow.

## Reopening Gate

Revisit this lane only after the DJ beta provides real evidence that:

- DJs can start and operate rooms without confusion.
- Patrons can complete Request, Tip, and Boost actions reliably.
- Paid request status reaches a trustworthy fulfilled state.
- Payments, refunds, disputes, and payouts behave correctly.
- DJs actually want independent or exclusive tracks inside the request flow.
- Artists show real interest in supplying music under controlled terms.
- The product team can verify track identity, ownership claims, and fulfilled requests without creating unsafe payment behavior.

## Open Decisions for Post-Beta Review

The following points remain intentionally undecided:

- Final public product names.
- Default Sway First duration.
- Whether Sway Only launches at all in the first music release.
- Exact Artist Share formula.
- Preview length.
- Full-track listening rules.
- Download rules.
- DJ file-delivery method.
- Contributor acceptance and payout process.
- Track verification requirements.
- Actual-play verification standard.
- Takedown and dispute process.
- Refund treatment after a track has been fulfilled.
- Membership benefit funding rules.
- Sway platform fee for exclusive music transactions.

## Preserved Product Rule

> A future Sway artist may choose which tracks are Open, Sway First, Sway Only, Private, or Retired; control which rooms, DJs, events, and memberships may access them; retain ownership; and receive an approved share only when an authorized live request is actually fulfilled.

## Current Action

Keep this document shelved. Do not implement this lane during the opening DJ beta. Use the beta to prove the live money loop first.
