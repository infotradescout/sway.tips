# Sway.DIO economic model (governing lock)

**Status:** Owner-locked product truth for the planned Sway.DIO streaming layer. Do not contradict in docs, readiness, AGENTS, marketing, or release language.

**Lane:** Self-Production → Sway.DIO (planned). This lock does **not** authorize Sway.DIO launch, live Stripe for Live Rooms, DSP delivery, streaming/royalty runtime, merge, or deploy. See `docs/SWAY_PRODUCT_STRUCTURE.md`.

## Bottom line (decision D)

Sway.DIO is the **streaming side of Self-Production**.

Funding model: **all three** — listener subscriptions, advertising, sponsorships — **staged**:

- **Private beta / first earnable streams:** subscription-funded **Sway Exclusives** only (clear funding source)
- **Later:** add advertising-funded and sponsor-funded listening without changing the zero streaming-cut rule
- **Forever:** 100% of streaming income attributable to qualifying Sway Exclusive artists goes to them; Sway takes **$0** streaming cut

For now only Sway Exclusives qualify to earn from streams.

## Competitive offer

Keep ownership. Release through Sway. Stream on Sway.DIO. Receive 100% of stream money. Sway makes money helping the career grow, not taking music income.

## Sway Exclusive ≠ ownership

Sway Exclusive means exclusive home / distributor / business partner for specific releases. The artist keeps the master. It is not ownership of the artist or their catalog.
